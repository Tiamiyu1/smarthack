  **Wheel Spinner** is a free online tool that allows you to create and customize spinning wheels for random selections, perfect for giveaways, decision-making, and classroom activities.
  
  Wheel Spinner (Wheel of Names) is an interactive tool that lets you create customizable spinning wheels for selecting names, numbers, or any other options at random. Whether you're running a giveaway, making decisions, or engaging students in a fun activity, this tool provides an easy and visually engaging way to generate random results.

**Key features**
- Simple and intuitive interface.
- Customizable colors, sounds, and spin duration.
- Free to use with no account required.
- Great for giveaways, classroom activities, and team decisions.

**How It Works**
- Enter the names or options you want on the wheel.
- Customize the appearance (colors, fonts, etc.).
- Click the wheel to spin and let chance decide the winner!
