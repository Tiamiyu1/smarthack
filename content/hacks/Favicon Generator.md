  **Favicon Generator** is a free online tool that creates high-quality favicons from text, images, or emojis, perfect for branding your website.
  
  Favicon Generator (favicon.io) helps you easily generate favicons for your website using text, images, or emojis. A favicon is the small icon that appears in browser tabs, bookmarks, and mobile home screens, enhancing your site's branding and recognition.


**Personal use case:** 
> The favicon for this site ('SH') was created using the text-to-favicon tool, making it quick and effortless to brand Smart Hack.

**Key features**
- Free and easy-to-use interface
- Generates favicons in multiple formats (.ico, .png, .svg) for different devices
- No design skills required—simply enter text and customize
- Helps improve website branding and user experience

**How it works**
- Choose your favicon type: text, image, or emoji
- Customize the font, background, and colors
- Generate and download your favicon package
