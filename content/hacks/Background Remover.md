  **Remove.bg** is an AI-powered online tool that instantly removes backgrounds from images with just one click. 
  
  It is a powerful online tool designed to automatically remove backgrounds from images in seconds. Using advanced artificial intelligence, it precisely detects subjects and separates them from their backgrounds, making it ideal for designers, marketers, and everyday users who need transparent images for presentations, social media, or creative projects. The platform supports various file formats and allows users to download high-quality cutouts. Remove.bg also offers an API for automation and integration into workflows, enhancing productivity for businesses and professionals alike.


**Personal use case:** 
 > I was helping my sister with her university registration but none of her digital passports was available in the required background colour. I used the platform to create a transparent version of her favourite passport, made the required background colour and also got two other backgrounds for future use.

**Key features**
- Background removal (to transparent)
- Saves time and provides high-quality image cutouts without manual editing
- Change background colour

**How it works**
- Upload an image and let the AI remove the background automatically
