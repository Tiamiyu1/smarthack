  **Image Resizer** is simple online tool that allows you to resize, crop, and optimize images quickly without losing quality.

  It is a free and easy-to-use online tool designed for resizing images to specific dimensions while maintaining their quality. It supports various formats, including JPEG, PNG, and GIF, and allows users to adjust width, height, and aspect ratio. The tool is useful for optimizing images for websites, social media, and presentations. Some versions also offer bulk resizing, compression, and format conversion, making it ideal for professionals and casual users alike.


**Personal use case:** 
> The Open Graph image (OG image) for this website was generated by resizing the favicon to match the required pixel dimensions.

**Key features**
- Resize image to any stretch while maintaining the original quality
- Fast, free, and easy, supporting multiple image formats and bulk resizing
- Change background colour

**How it works**
- Upload your image, set the desired size, and download the resized version
